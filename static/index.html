One service, five retrieval configurations, every choice measured. Sparse BM25 stored as a term-weight matrix, a MiniLM encoder fine-tuned on MS MARCO over an HNSW index, reciprocal rank fusion, and a cross-encoder reranker. Gemini writes the answer from the retrieved passages and must cite each claim; a judge from a different model family grades faithfulness and correctness.

Design: docs/design.md. Build notes: docs/build-log.md. Findings: docs/findings.md. Source on GitHub: abrasive519mars/rag-evals-demo.

Citations open the passage below the answer. Repeated questions are served from an exact-match cache and marked as such.
